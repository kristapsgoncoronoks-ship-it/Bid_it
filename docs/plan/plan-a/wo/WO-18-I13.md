## Objective and business value

`backend/app/services/cash_position.py:100-113::summary()` computes `net_position = receivables.outstanding − payables.outstanding` — a **working-capital gap**, not a bank balance (no bank-account entity exists anywhere in `backend/app/models/`; `grep -rn "class.*Account\b" backend/app/models/` finds none that represent a cash/bank ledger). `backend/app/services/cash_flow.py:1-9::monthly()` is confirmed **strictly backward-looking**: it buckets settled `Payment`/`SupplierPayment`/`ReimbursementBatch` rows into the trailing N months up to `today` (`_month_window`, `paid_on >= start` where `start` is the oldest of the trailing window) — there is no forward projection.

`frontend/src/pages/CashPosition.tsx` currently presents both figures without disclosing either limitation:
- Line 34-37: page header — `"Cash position" / "Receivables, payables, and bank reconciliation at a glance. Net EUR."` — no mention that this is not a bank balance.
- Line 54-59: the headline `StatCard label="Net position"` with only a directional sub ("receivables exceed payables" / "payables exceed receivables") — reads exactly like a bank-balance summary tile to anyone skimming the page, which is precisely the ARCH_plan-flagged risk.
- Line 63-67: `<Card title="Cash flow (last 12 months)">` wrapping `<CashFlowChart>` — a finance user glancing at a line chart titled "Cash flow" with no time-direction cue may reasonably read the rightmost points as a forward trend, when every point is settled history.

Precedent already exists and is tested: `frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx:121-129` (built in WO-16 / I1.1) labels the equivalent home-dashboard tile **"Working-capital gap"** with sub `"receivables − payables — not a bank balance"`, asserted by `frontend/e2e/dashboard.spec.ts:132-135` (`cashTile).toContainText("not a bank balance")`). I1.3 finishes the job WO-16 deliberately left open: the *detail page* itself (`/cash-position`), which is what a user reaches by clicking that tile, still uses the pre-honesty copy.

Who pays: a trial prospect (or their bookkeeper) who clicks through the dashboard tile lands on a page headlined "Net position: €1,480.00" with no caveat — the single most literal way ARCH risk row 24 could bite a design partner mid-trial ("where is this cash — show me the bank account"). This is a same-day, zero-risk fix (pure copy + one new small paragraph, no backend change, no wire-shape change) that closes the last named M1 UI-honesty gap before M1's exit criteria can be called clean.

### Scope

**In scope:**

  - The headline "Net position" `StatCard` (line 54-59): relabel to **"Working-capital gap"** (matches the already-shipped, already-tested Dashboard-tile wording exactly — one honest vocabulary across the app, not two), sub text keeps its existing directional message and appends **"— not a bank balance"**.

- The "Bank reconciliation" card on the same page — it already accurately describes imported bank-statement *lines* (matched/unmatched/ignored), not a balance; ARCH_plan risk row 22 ("reconciliation annotates but never posts cash") is a separate PAY-LATER item with no "relabel now" instruction attached, unlike rows 23/24.
