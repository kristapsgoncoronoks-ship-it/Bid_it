### Objective and business value

Verified at HEAD=`e5bff7d`: `fuel_card_parser._default_parsers()`
registers exactly `[EurowagParser(), E100Parser(), Q8Parser(),
DKVParser(), TFCParser()]`; no Moeve parser exists, so a Moeve
(ex-Cepsa) statement has no path into the claim engine at all
(`select()` raises `unrecognized_fuel_card_statement`).
`BA_fleet_fuel.md` §5.1's Moeve row (line 1102) gives the network's
harvested quirks verbatim: *"Moeve Pro Services S.A.U. (ex-Cepsa); UAB
«Client-LT-2»; EUR; monthly; **ALL amounts VAT-inclusive**; per-line
IVA rate (10% gasoleo / 21% EcoBlue); cash-at-pump nets against
transfer; 6-dp internal calc."* — and §4.2 (line 1011) classifies the
MOEVE "PRN off pump PVP" discount as **ON-INVOICE** ("already inside
`net_eur`"). This is a VARIATION of E100's reverse VAT-inclusive-gross
model (WO-63 built the code path precisely so Moeve could follow), with
THREE Moeve-only properties: (1) the per-line IVA rate genuinely VARIES
line-by-line within one statement (10% Spanish gasoleo next to 21%
EcoBlue — E100's fixtures never mixed rates, and the WO-66 gate's
harvested ES dual entry `(21, 10)` exists exactly for this network's
coherence); (2) the derivation runs at the harvested **6-dp internal
calc** — `net_local` is quantized to 6 decimal places inside the parser
(the supplier's own printed working precision) with `vat_local = gross
− net` exact by construction, and `q2` still happens ONLY at the ingest
boundary; (3) a per-line **cash-at-pump settlement flag** whose
harvested semantics ("nets against transfer"; §4.2's note column:
"invoice number ‖ rebate explanation ‖ cash-at-pump flag") this order
must place correctly — see the DOCUMENTED DECISION below. All of that
arithmetic is parser-local: the derived `net_local`/`vat_local`/
`gross_local` ride the EXISTING `ParsedFuelLine` fields and every
fixture line is EUR so ingestion's untouched identity branch applies —
the shared contract does not change (WO-65's stability argument,
honored by construction).

Who stops losing money: every Moeve statement for the
«Client-LT-2»-shaped Lithuanian entity currently has NO path to a VAT
claim — unrecoverable Spanish VAT for any fleet fueling on the
Moeve/Cepsa network. Worse, a naive port that applied one flat rate
(the E100-fixture habit) would mis-split every 10%-gasoleo line's
net/VAT (overstating the reclaimable VAT by 11 points of gross — a
wrong figure feeding a legal filing), and one that dropped or
double-counted cash-at-pump lines would corrupt either the claim (a
cash-paid litre is still invoiced, VAT-bearing fuel) or the settlement
picture. Deriving per line from the printed gross + printed IVA rate —
and refusing any row whose rate or settlement channel is unreadable
rather than guessing — is exactly the "every promise dies if a number
is wrong" discipline.

### THE CASH-AT-PUMP DECISION (documented, per the session brief)

**A cash-at-pump row IS a `ParsedFuelLine` — never a coversheet
adjustment.** The harvested facts force this: §4.2 places the
cash-at-pump flag in the per-LINE note column (alongside the invoice
number), i.e. the flagged row is an invoiced transaction line that
appears on the statement with its own gross and IVA — the fuel was
bought, invoiced, and its VAT is exactly as reclaimable as any other
line's. What "nets against transfer" describes is SETTLEMENT: the
monthly bank transfer pays the invoice total MINUS what was already
paid in cash at the pump. Settlement is not capture — modelling the
transfer amount is M4 (payments/cash depth) and expectation-vs-activity
is G3.5 receipt-control territory; neither may leak into the claim
engine. Concretely:
- the flag rides `provenance_note` (WO-50's note-split assigned
  provenance exactly this channel; `ParsedFuelLine` is frozen, and a
  typed flag would widen the contract — the design error TASK SELECTION
  (e) names);
- the invoiced figures of a cash-at-pump line are UNCHANGED (same
  reverse calc, same claim path, `net_eur_eff == net_eur` — the flag
  never mutates a number);
- the batch tie-out ties Σ(net+vat) of ALL lines against the
  STATEMENT/INVOICE total the operator captures (`coversheet_total`) —
  NOT the transfer amount. A statement whose cash lines were excluded
  from the coversheet figure will (correctly) fail the tie-out: the
  invoice document total is the only figure R25's gate ever tied.
- the parser surfaces ONE advisory warning when cash-at-pump lines
  exist (count + their gross sum, "nets against the bank transfer —
  settlement-side only"), so the review surface shows the netting
  figure without any number changing. Warnings never block (§2.1a).

### Implementation guidance

1. **The per-line-IVA VAT-inclusive money model (the reason this slice
   exists).** The harvested facts are: ALL amounts VAT-inclusive; the
   per-line IVA rate (10% gasoleo / 21% EcoBlue); the 6-dp internal
   calc; the cash-at-pump netting; EUR; the ON-INVOICE PRN discount
   (inside the printed gross). Everything else is a DOCUMENTED
   INTERPRETATION flagged in the module docstring (no worked Moeve
   column layout exists in the spec — the same discipline as every
   prior parser's shape choice): the statement prints the VAT-inclusive
   `gross_local`, the per-line `iva_rate`, and a per-line `payment`
   channel. Derive: `net_local = q(gross / (1 + rate/100),
   Decimal("0.000001"))` — the ONE place the 6-dp harvested quirk
   lives; `vat_local = gross − net_local` EXACT, so the
   `net + vat == gross` identity holds to the last digit and the
   implied rate stays within rule 9's ±0.5pp window (e.g. gross 100.00
   at 21% → net 82.644628, vat 17.355372, implied 21.0000001…%).
   `fuel_ingest.ingest_transaction` stays the one place that `q2`s
   (WO-50's convention). E100's fully-unrounded derivation is NOT
   changed — the 6-dp step is Moeve's harvested quirk, not a shared
   convention.
2. **Rate policy lives in the gate, not the parser.** `iva_rate` is
   bounded `[0, 100]` (the E100 defensive bound — out of range refuses
   the whole statement); there is NO `{10, 21}` whitelist. A statement
   claiming 15% on an ES line parses (the arithmetic is
   self-consistent) and the WO-66 gate's rule 9 — whose harvested ES
   dual entry `(21, 10)` exists for this network — flags it
   `vat_rate_incoherent` (warn, never blocks; §2.1a). The ingest suite
   asserts BOTH directions: 10%/21% ES lines produce NO coherence
   warning; a 15% ES line produces one and still registers.
3. **The `payment` channel fails CLOSED; the flag never touches a
   number.** Normalize case-insensitively to `transfer`/`cash_at_pump`;
   ANY other value aborts the whole statement (a guessed settlement
   channel would corrupt the future M4 netting picture the same way a
   guessed tier corrupts a TFC net). A `cash_at_pump` line's derived
   figures are byte-identical to a `transfer` line's; the flag appends
   to `provenance_note` and the parse emits the ONE advisory
   cash-at-pump summary warning (count + gross sum) described in THE
   CASH-AT-PUMP DECISION.
4. **The WO-66 gate is upstream of ingestion — fixtures must pass it.**
   Every factory row carries an `invoice_ref` (gate rule 1, error), and
   the default fixture is ES-only at 10/21 (rule-9 coherent under the
   harvested ES dual entry). The ingest suite must include the
   live-gate onboarding pair over DERIVED figures: with explicit rows
   whose grosses are exact (gasoleo 660.00 @10, EcoBlue 121.00 @21,
   gasoleo cash-at-pump 220.00 @10), Σ(net+vat) = Σ gross = 1001.00
   exactly (the VAT-inclusive identity — the tie total IS the invoice
   total, cash lines included per THE CASH-AT-PUMP DECISION) — a
   coversheet of 1001.02 registers (diff €0.02) and 1001.03 blocks
   (`capture_review_blocked`, zero rows).
5. **No entity detection, ever, for Moeve.** Mirror
   `q8.py`/`dkv.py`/`tfc.py` exactly: no scan exists in the module;
   `entities` is unconditionally `[]`; ONE warning per parse using the
   "not implemented for this network" wording; an adversarial test
   plants a "Moeve Pro Services S.A.U. ... VAT: ES9…"-shaped decoy line
   (all-nines synthetic body, allow-listed in the same commit) and
   asserts `entities` stays empty.
6. **Registration + the one alignment.** `_default_parsers()` appends
   `MoeveParser()` sixth. Six distinct literal first-line markers ⇒
   registration order remains provably irrelevant. The TFC suite's
   exact-five-list registry test is aligned to membership + relative
   order (comment pointing at this order, mirroring the WO-67 comment
   in `test_g3_2_dkv_fuel_card_parser.py`); the exact six-list is
   asserted in the Moeve suite. Every OTHER WO-62..67 test passes
   byte-for-byte unmodified.

### Invariants this order must preserve

- **§4.9 (Decimal, never float)** — the reverse calc, the 6-dp
  quantization (`app.core.money.q`, ROUND_HALF_UP) and the exact
  `vat = gross − net` are pure `Decimal`; the ONE 2-dp rounding point
  stays `ingest_transaction.q2`.
- **§4.10 (the server recomputes every total)** — the parser recomputes
  net/VAT from the printed VAT-inclusive gross and printed rate;
  nothing client-supplied is trusted as a net/VAT split.
- **§4.14/§4.15 (one FX convention)** — untouched by construction:
  every fixture line is EUR and rides the existing identity branch; a
  hypothetical non-EUR Moeve line would ride the existing ECB branch
  unchanged; no FX code changes.
- **§4.19 (R25 gate semantics)** — the gate is not modified; this order
  only proves a NEW network's fixtures against it (the €0.02/€0.03 pair
  through `ingest_statement`) and REUSES its rule-9 ES dual entry
  instead of duplicating rate policy in the parser.
- **Tenancy (§4.1-4.5)** — same two adversarial shapes every transport
  slice proves (cross-tenant opaque 404 via `NotFoundError`,
  overlapping-data isolation), proven again for Moeve's statement
  shape.
- **§10 (no invented functionality)** — settlement modelling,
  product→rate policy, PRN-discount verification, cadence enforcement
  and entity detection are refused in "Out of scope" with their owning
  board ids, not silently attempted.
- **ADR-P3 (transport isolation)** — the new parser imports only
  `fuel_card_parser` types + `app.core.money`; `test_boundaries.py`
  stays green.
