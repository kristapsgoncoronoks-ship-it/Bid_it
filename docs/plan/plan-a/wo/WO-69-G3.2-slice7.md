### Objective and business value

Verified at HEAD=`428ae27`: `fuel_card_parser._default_parsers()`
registers exactly `[EurowagParser(), E100Parser(), Q8Parser(),
DKVParser(), TFCParser(), MoeveParser()]`; no BP/Aral parser exists, so
a BP/Aral statement has no path into the claim engine at all
(`select()` raises `unrecognized_fuel_card_statement`).
`BA_fleet_fuel.md` §5.1's BP/Aral row (line 1099) gives the network's
harvested quirks verbatim: *"BP / Aral (B2Mobility GmbH); SIA
«Client-LV»; PLN; monthly; Poland **split-payment (MPP) mandatory**; A2
toll ~2.5% ORS fee lines; FX via dated ECB rate with `month_config.FX`
fallback."* Unlike TFC (derived net) or Moeve/E100 (reverse
VAT-inclusive), nothing in the harvested row says BP's figures are
anything but independently printed — the money model is Eurowag/Q8's
shape (net_local/vat_local/gross_local read verbatim off the file), and
the network's REAL quirks are all elsewhere: (1) it is the first
ALL-PLN network, so ingestion's ECB branch (`fx.to_eur` at the line's
own `txn_date`, `fx_source="ecb"`) is the DEFAULT path rather than
Q8's one-line exception; (2) the mandatory Polish split-payment (MPP)
annotation, which is a SETTLEMENT mechanic this order must place
correctly (see THE MPP DECISION below); (3) the ~2.5% ORS fee lines
riding on A2-toll transactions, whose mapping (fuel line vs fee line vs
coversheet adjustment) this order must decide and document (see THE
ORS-FEE-LINE DECISION below). All of it is parser-local: the printed
figures ride the EXISTING `ParsedFuelLine` fields, PLN rides the
EXISTING ECB branch, and the shared contract does not change.

Who stops losing money: every BP/Aral statement for the
«Client-LV»-shaped Latvian entity currently has NO path to a VAT claim
— unrecoverable Polish VAT (23% on fuel, 8% on A2 tolls) for any fleet
fueling on the BP/Aral network. Worse, the two mapping mistakes a naive
port invites are both financially corrosive: treating the ORS fee line
as a coversheet adjustment (or dropping it) breaks the batch tie-out
against the invoice total AND forfeits the fee's own reclaimable 23%
VAT; and letting the MPP settlement mechanic leak into capture (e.g.
splitting the VAT out of the stored figures because the PAYMENT is
split) would corrupt every claim figure — the invoice's net/vat/gross
are unchanged by how the payment is routed. Reading the printed figures
verbatim, refusing any row whose line kind is unreadable, and keeping
MPP strictly settlement-side is the "every promise dies if a number is
wrong" discipline.

### THE ORS-FEE-LINE DECISION (documented, per the session brief)

**An ORS fee line IS a `ParsedFuelLine` — an ordinary VAT-bearing
statement line; never a coversheet adjustment, never merged into its
toll line.** The harvested facts force this: §5.1 calls them "~2.5% ORS
**fee lines**" — LINES on the statement — and §4.2's canonical
transaction schema explicitly models non-fuel statement lines
(`product_group` `Toll/Fees` and `Service/Other`; `qty` is "units
(AdBlue, parking, tolls)"); the closest harvested precedent, DKV's
5.63% service fee on parking/services, is classified ON-INVOICE
("already inside `net_eur`", §4.2) — a fee the network invoices is a
VAT-bearing line ON the invoice document, inside the invoice total that
R25's tie-out ties. Concretely:
- an ORS fee line carries its own printed net/vat/gross (23% Polish
  standard rate — rule 9's PL entry coheres it) and its VAT is exactly
  as reclaimable as any other line's — under Art. 9 goods codes it maps
  through `product_group` `"Service/Other"` → code 10 (Other), while
  its A2-toll sibling maps through `"Toll/Fees"` → code 4 (road tolls);
  merging the fee into the toll line would misstate BOTH goods codes on
  a legal filing;
- the batch tie-out ties Σ(net+vat) of ALL lines — fee lines included —
  against the STATEMENT/INVOICE total the operator captures
  (`coversheet_total`); a coversheet that excluded the fee lines will
  (correctly) fail the tie-out;
- the parser identifies the line kind via a per-line `line_type` column
  (`fuel` / `toll` / `ors_fee`), fail-CLOSED on any other value — a
  guessed line kind would silently mis-map the Art. 9 goods-code path
  (fuel=1 / tolls=4 / other=10) the same way a guessed TFC tier
  invents a discount or a guessed Moeve settlement channel corrupts
  the netting picture;
- the "~2.5% of the toll" relationship is a CONTRACT term (the "~"
  says it is approximate) — the parser NEVER verifies or recomputes it
  (verifying contracted terms is G3.4/M5 contract-audit territory,
  exactly like TFC's tier grant and DKV's 5.63% fee); instead the parse
  surfaces ONE advisory warning when ORS fee lines exist (count +
  per-currency gross sum, alongside the toll lines' per-currency gross
  sum, so a reviewer can eyeball the contracted ratio without any
  number changing). Warnings never block (§2.1a).

### THE MPP DECISION (documented, per the session brief)

**Split-payment (MPP) is SETTLEMENT — the parser records the
annotation, and it never touches a figure.** Poland's mechanizm
podzielonej płatności routes the VAT portion of the PAYMENT into the
supplier's dedicated VAT account; the invoice's printed net/vat/gross
are what they always were, and the legally-required observable at
CAPTURE time is the "mechanizm podzielonej płatności" annotation
printed on the invoice document. Modelling the split payment itself
(two payment legs, the VAT account) is M4 payments/cash territory;
expectation-vs-activity is G3.5 receipt-control territory — neither may
leak into the claim engine, the same boundary THE CASH-AT-PUMP DECISION
drew for Moeve in WO-68. Concretely:
- the statement format carries the annotation as a statement-level
  literal line (`MECHANIZM PODZIELONEJ PŁATNOŚCI` — the statutory
  wording; see the format interpretation below); the parser scans for
  the literal, never a fuzzy match;
- annotation PRESENT ⇒ ONE advisory warning stating the per-currency
  VAT total the mechanism directs to the dedicated VAT account
  ("settlement-side only; the invoiced figures and the VAT claim are
  unchanged") — per-currency, never a raw cross-currency sum
  (master-context §4.14);
- annotation ABSENT ⇒ ONE advisory warning ("no MPP annotation found,
  though Polish split-payment is mandatory for this network — review
  the source document"). This fails OPEN deliberately: an absent
  settlement annotation corrupts no captured figure, so blocking
  registration on it would invent policy (contrast the `line_type`
  refusal above, where a present-but-unknown enumerated VALUE is
  unreadable data). Warnings never block (§2.1a);
- no per-line flag, no `ParsedFuelLine` widening, no figure change —
  the flag never exists as data, only as the two advisory wordings.

**The `month_config.FX` fallback is deliberately NOT reproduced.** The
harvested row says "FX via dated ECB rate with `month_config.FX`
fallback" — the dated-ECB-rate part IS ingestion's existing ECB branch
(`fx.to_eur` at the line's own `txn_date`); the static-fallback-table
part is the retired system's compromise and would violate
master-context §4.15 verbatim ("`unknown` yields `NULL`, never a
guessed number") and §4.2's own "no coverage ⇒ NULL is stored, never a
fabricated pass". The platform refuses (`fx_rate_unavailable`, zero
rows, the two-phase guarantee) instead of falling back — the STRICTER
of the two harvested rules wins, and this order proves that refusal
over BP's own fixture shape.

### Implementation guidance

1. **The money model is deliberately the SIMPLE one.** The harvested
   row gives BP no derivation quirk — figures are independently given
   (Eurowag/Q8's shape). Read `net_local`/`vat_local`/`gross_local`
   verbatim as unrounded Decimals; the ONE 2-dp rounding point stays
   `fuel_ingest.ingest_transaction`'s `q2`; the PLN→EUR conversion is
   ingestion's EXISTING ECB branch (`fx.to_eur` at each line's own
   `txn_date`, both amounts at the SAME resolved rate,
   `fx_source="ecb"`, `fx_rate`/`fx_ecb_rate`/`fx_ecb_date` frozen).
   The parser must NOT pre-convert, NOT enforce PLN, and NOT reproduce
   the retired fallback table (see the documented decision).
2. **`line_type` fails CLOSED; the two DECISIONS are advisory-only.**
   Normalize `line_type` case-insensitively to `fuel`/`toll`/`ors_fee`;
   any other value aborts the whole statement. The MPP annotation scan
   is a statement-level literal (`MECHANIZM PODZIELONEJ PŁATNOŚCI`);
   present ⇒ the per-currency-VAT advisory, absent ⇒ the
   mandatory-network advisory — both warnings, never blocks, no figure
   change. The ORS advisory (count + per-currency ORS gross + per-
   currency toll gross) is emitted once, only when ORS lines exist.
   Per-currency totals, never a raw cross-currency sum (§4.14).
3. **Rate policy lives in the gate, not the parser.** No VAT-rate
   whitelist, no line_type→rate mapping: the WO-66 gate's rule 9
   carries the HARVESTED PL dual entry `(23, 8)` — the default
   fixture's 23% fuel / 8% toll / 23% ORS mix coheres with NO
   `vat_rate_incoherent` finding (asserted), and rule-9 policy is
   reused, never duplicated (the same argument WO-68 documented for
   ES).
4. **The WO-66 gate is upstream of ingestion — fixtures must pass it,
   and the tie is DOCUMENT-currency.** Every factory row carries an
   `invoice_ref` (gate rule 1). The tie-out compares Σ(net_local+
   vat_local) against `coversheet_total` — for an all-PLN statement the
   coversheet figure is the PLN invoice total. The live-gate onboarding
   pair uses explicit exact rows: fuel 1000.00+230.00 @23, toll
   200.00+16.00 @8, ORS fee 5.00+1.15 @23 (the fee = 2.5% of the toll
   net, matching the harvested ratio) ⇒ Σ(net+vat) = 1452.15 PLN
   exactly; a coversheet of 1452.17 registers (diff 0.02) and 1452.18
   blocks (`capture_review_blocked`, zero rows).
5. **The ECB path is the headline, and the refusal is proven.** The
   registration test loads a dated PLN rate (`fx.load_rates`, e.g. 4.30
   on the fixture dates), asserts `fx_source="ecb"`, `fx_rate==4.30`,
   `net_eur`/`vat_eur` = local/4.30 q2-rounded, `net_eur_eff ==
   net_eur` for every row, and the `product_group` mapping the ORS
   decision predicts (`Toll/Fees` for the toll row, `Service/Other`
   for the ORS row, `Diesel` for the fuel row). The two-phase test
   pairs a clean EUR line with a PLN line and loads NO rate ⇒
   `fx_rate_unavailable`, zero rows (the clean line must also not be
   written).
6. **No entity detection, ever, for BP.** Mirror `q8.py`/`dkv.py`/
   `tfc.py`/`moeve.py` exactly: no scan exists in the module;
   `entities` is unconditionally `[]`; ONE warning per parse using the
   "not implemented for this network" wording; an adversarial test
   plants a "B2Mobility GmbH ... VAT: PL9…"-shaped decoy line
   (all-nines synthetic body, allow-listed in the same commit) and
   asserts `entities` stays empty.
7. **Registration + the one alignment.** `_default_parsers()` appends
   `BPParser()` seventh. Seven distinct literal first-line markers ⇒
   registration order remains provably irrelevant. The Moeve suite's
   exact-six-list registry test is aligned to membership + relative
   order (comment pointing at this order, mirroring the WO-68 comment
   in `test_g3_2_tfc_fuel_card_parser.py`); the exact seven-list is

### Invariants this order must preserve

- **§4.9 (Decimal, never float)** — figures are read as `Decimal`
  verbatim; no parser-side arithmetic beyond the advisory per-currency
  sums (pure Decimal); the ONE 2-dp rounding point stays
  `ingest_transaction.q2`.
- **§4.10 (the server recomputes every total)** — the batch tie-out
  recomputes Σ(net+vat) against the operator-captured coversheet; the
  EUR figures are recomputed by ingestion's ECB branch from the local
  figures — nothing client-supplied is trusted as an EUR amount.
- **§4.14 (no aggregate sums across currencies)** — the MPP and ORS
  advisory totals are PER-CURRENCY by construction, never a raw
  cross-currency sum.
- **§4.15 (one FX convention)** — PLN converts by DIVIDING by the
  dated ECB rate via the existing branch; no cached rate ⇒
  `fx_rate_unavailable` refusal, zero rows — the harvested
  `month_config.FX` fallback is REFUSED as a documented decision, not
  silently skipped.
- **§4.19 (R25 gate semantics)** — the gate is not modified; this order
  only proves the seventh network's fixtures against it (the
  0.02/0.03 pair over PLN document-currency totals) and REUSES rule
  9's harvested PL dual entry instead of duplicating rate policy.
- **Tenancy (§4.1-4.5)** — the same two adversarial shapes every
  transport slice proves (cross-tenant opaque 404 via `NotFoundError`,
  overlapping-data isolation), proven again for BP's statement shape.
- **§10 (no invented functionality)** — split-payment settlement
  modelling, ORS contract verification, a fallback FX table, cadence
  enforcement and entity detection are refused in "Out of scope" with
  their owning board ids, not silently attempted.
- **ADR-P3 (transport isolation)** — the new parser imports only
  `fuel_card_parser` types; `test_boundaries.py` stays green.
