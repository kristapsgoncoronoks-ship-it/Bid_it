### Steps
1. Lifecycle service: draft (editable) → approved (review gate, still no number) →
   issue (validate completeness incl. Art. 226 fields, allocate number under the G-8
   lock, FREEZE seller_json/buyer_json, stamp issued_at) → the post-issue events:
   dispute/undispute, write_off, cancel (draft/approved only), void rules per spec.
   Only a DRAFT may be edited: `edit` on any other lifecycle → 409 with the
   "correct it with a credit note" message.
2. VAT: `app/services/vat.py` — per-line net = q2(qty×unit_price×(1−disc)); bucket
   lines by rate; round at BUCKET level (q2 per rate subtotal); schemes: standard,
   reverse-charge (0%, mandatory note + buyer VAT required), intra-EU, exempt
   (reason required). Server recomputes EVERYTHING; client totals ignored (test:
   post wrong totals → recomputed persisted).
3. Credit notes: build from original (full or partial), SAME frozen seller, own legal
   series, over-crediting refused under the issuer lock: sum(credits) ≤ original total;
   `credited_total` maintained; a fully-credited invoice derives status `credited`.
4. Derived-never-stored payment status: open|partial|paid|overdue computed from
   amount_paid, credited_total, due_date. NO status column for these (test asserts
   column absence).
5. Duplicate-as-draft; every transition audited + evented.
