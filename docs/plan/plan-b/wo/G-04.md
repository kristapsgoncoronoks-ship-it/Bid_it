## G-4 — Structural authorization (deny-by-default, both-direction CI)

**Effort M (4–6d) · P0 · Depends: G-3.** Prepend `shared/00_MASTER_CONTEXT.md`.
Lesson encoded from the old platform: per-handler `require()` calls left whole routers
unguarded (vendors, partners). Here enforcement is STRUCTURAL from the first route.

### Scope
IN: permission vocabulary, role→permission matrix, route-dependency enforcement,
coverage CI, audit of denials. OUT: SSO/SCIM; fine-grained object ACLs.

### Steps
1. `app/core/authz.py`: `Permission` StrEnum (seed: ORG_MANAGE, MEMBER_MANAGE,
   AR_READ/WRITE/ISSUE/SEND, AP_READ/WRITE/APPROVE, PAYMENT_READ/WRITE/APPROVE,
   EXPENSE_*, DOC_*, BILLING_MANAGE, AUDIT_READ — extend per context as they land);
   `ROLE_PERMISSIONS` matrix for owner/admin/member/viewer; `has_perm(role, perm)`.
   Deny-by-default: unknown role or perm → False.
2. Enforcement as a ROUTER DEPENDENCY: `require(perm)` returns a FastAPI dependency;
   every APIRouter is CONSTRUCTED with a base permission dependency; mutating routes
   declare their specific permission. Keep `authz` free of `app.api` imports (the
   dependency lives in `app/api/deps.py`, calling core) — the boundary test stays intact.
3. Coverage CI, both directions: walk `app.routes`; (a) every route must resolve to at
   least one permission dependency (allowlist: /healthz, /auth/login, /auth/refresh);
   (b) every declared permission must exist in the matrix. Fail on any gap.
4. Maker≠checker primitive now (used later by payments/vendor changes):
   `require_different_actor(created_by)` helper + test.
5. Every denial is audit-logged (actor, route, permission) once G-5 lands — leave a
   TODO hook wired to a no-op logger until then.

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A new route added WITHOUT a permission fails CI (prove in a scratch commit; revert).
- [ ] Viewer role: every mutating route returns 403 (parameterized test over the route table).
- [ ] Unknown permission string in a dependency → startup error, not silent allow.
